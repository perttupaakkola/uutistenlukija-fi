# SEO Keyword Research — 8 Launch Categories

**Author:** Monica | **Date:** 2026-03-22 | **Status:** Complete

## Core Strategy

The best category-page SEO play is:

1. **Use clear Finnish category slugs**
2. Optimize for **category + modifier** searches, not just broad `uutiset`
3. Use category pages as **freshness hubs**
4. Support them with **topic/entity pages** for long-tail capture
