# Partnership & Syndication Opportunities — uutistenlukija.fi (2026-03)

**Last updated:** 2026-03-22
**Purpose:** Research and map realistic partnership and syndication opportunities for uutistenlukija.fi at launch and post-launch stages.

---

## Executive summary

Uutistenlukija.fi has three realistic partnership tracks:

1. **Distribution partnerships** — getting uutistenlukija listed, indexed, and promoted via platform channels (Ampparit, Google News, Apple News)
2. **Content/source partnerships** — building sustainable, legally clear ingest relationships with Finnish publishers via RSS/feed agreements
3. **Tech partnerships** — selecting and connecting to the right CDN, analytics, ad network, and newsletter tools to operate efficiently

A fourth track — **direct content-sharing or cross-promotion agreements** with Finnish publishers — is a realistic medium-term goal but unlikely to be accessible at launch given the copyright posture and legal risk environment. The door-opening strategy for this is to demonstrate traffic value first.

---

## 1) FINNISH MEDIA PARTNERSHIPS — CONTENT SHARING & CROSS-PROMOTION

### Bottom line

Formal content-sharing or co-publishing partnerships with major Finnish news publishers are **not a realistic day-one play** for an early-stage verkkolehti, given:

- the publisher-rights / Article 15 legal environment (covered in the legal brief)
- Uutiskeräin.fi's closure in Apr 2025 as a cautionary precedent
- the need to first prove traffic and audience value
- the need to establish credibility as a new publication

However, **two forms of informal or light-touch relationship are achievable**:

### A. Cross-promotion with smaller / regional publishers

Smaller Finnish regional papers, specialist blogs, and independent outlets may be more open to informal reciprocal linking or referral arrangements than large nationals.

**Target types:**

- Regional papers (Turun Sanomat, Karjalainen, Kaleva if feeds are fixed)
- Specialist content sites (pelaaja.fi, Tekniikka & Talous if re-verified)
- Independent Finnish-language analysis and commentary outlets
- Health and science content from Duodecim / Terveyskirjasto (clearly non-commercial educational)

**Approach:**

- uutistenlukija sends traffic → publishers see referral value in analytics
- uutistenlukija reaches out with referral data once traffic baseline is established
- cross-promotion starts informally (they tweet about it, we feature them prominently)
- formal arrangement only if both parties see value

**Timeline:** realistic 3-6 months post-launch when referral data exists

### B. STT / wire-service positioning

**STT** (Suomen Tietotoimisto — Finnish News Agency) is Finland's primary news wire. STT content is licensed to Finnish media outlets and powers much of the baseline news production.

STT does not typically have a direct self-service licensing model for small independent publishers, but understanding their licensing structure is worth tracking.

**Relevance:**

- Many Finnish news sites are STT subscribers — their stories appear widely
- Uutistenlukija using STT-sourced articles as research input for original journalism is low-risk (we write original articles, not republish)
- A future direct licensing discussion with STT for structured data / metadata is worth exploring at scale

**Timeline:** relevant at later stage; not a launch-day item

### C. International wire services for Finland-relevant international content

**AP (Associated Press)** and **Reuters** have regional offices and Nordic-audience relevance. Their English content is often free to access as a reader even if republication licensing is complex.

**Relevance:**

- Uutistenlukija's Ulkomaat category can be enriched with AP/Reuters-sourced stories from Finnish publishers who carry them
- We don't need a direct wire agreement as we write original articles based on publicly available sources

### D. Finnish media associations — not a formal partnership target but worth tracking

- **Medialiitto** (Finnish Media Federation) represents major publishers. More of an industry body than a partnership target, but staying aware of their positions on AI-generated content and publisher rights is useful.
- **Sanomalehtien Liitto** — similar relevance; monitors market norms

## 2) NEWS PLATFORM SUBMISSIONS

### A. Ampparit.com

**What Ampparit is:**

Ampparit.com is Finland's leading news aggregator, combining 250+ Finnish news sources. It operates on an RSS-feed-based model and is the closest existing comparable to uutistenlukija in the Finnish market.

**Submission process:**

Ampparit allows publishers/site owners to submit their site for inclusion. The submission flow (`ampparit.com/lisaa-sivusto`) was not publicly available in a plaintext form during this research session — the page appears to 404 on direct fetch, suggesting it may be behind a JS-rendered form or the URL has changed.

**Likely submission requirements (based on Ampparit's model):**

- active RSS feed
- Finnish-language content
- credible editorial identity
- passing a category review
- no spam / low-quality / content-farm status

**Why submit uutistenlukija to Ampparit:**

- inclusion = external link, discoverability, referral traffic
- signals legitimacy as a Finnish news source
- Ampparit is well-established enough that inclusion carries credibility
- it can also drive early audience without paid marketing

**Action items:**

- [ ] confirm uutistenlukija's own RSS/feed endpoint is live and well-formed before submission
- [ ] identify the correct submission route (form / email contact / editorial review)
- [ ] prepare submission materials: site description, category pitch, editorial mission statement
- [ ] submit after site is live and has at least 2-4 weeks of consistent content

**Important nuance:**

Uutistenlukija is a verkkolehti that produces original AI-written journalism — not an aggregator. This positions us well for Ampparit inclusion as an original content source. The best framing for submission would emphasize our original editorial content, categorized coverage, and quality-filtered journalism.

### B. Google News — Publisher Center

**What it is:**

Google News surfaces news content across:

- **Google News app** (topic feeds, local news)
- **Google Search "Top stories"** (news carousel in search results)
- **News tab in Google Search**
- **Google Discover** (personalised feed in mobile browsers)
- **Google News Showcase** (enhanced placement, requires partnership)

**Inclusion mechanics — critical finding from official Google documentation:**

_"Google may find your site through our normal web crawl."_
_"Publishers are automatically considered for 'Top stories' or the News tab of Search. They just need to produce high-quality content and comply with Google News content policies."_

This means:

- **Top stories and News tab** — automatic via web crawl. No active submission required. Just need quality content + news sitemap + Google News policies compliance.
- **Google News app** — primarily crawl-based, but Publisher Center registration helps control how content appears and how sections are organized
- **Google News Showcase** — requires a formal partnership agreement with Google, separate from standard inclusion

**Google Publisher Center:**

The Publisher Center (`publishercenter.google.com`) allows publishers to:

- register their publication formally
- configure publication name, logo, and section structure
- use Reader Revenue Manager for subscription sales
- track performance via Search Console integration

**Registration requires a Google account and editorial verification.** Uutistenlukija should register in Publisher Center even if technical inclusion is automatic, because it gives more control.

**Key requirements for Google News inclusion:**

From Google's content policies:

- [ ] clear bylines or authorship information
- [ ] transparent editorial process
- [ ] accurate dates on articles
- [ ] clean, well-structured HTML
- [ ] news sitemap (`<news:news>` schema)
- [ ] compliance with Google News content policies (no hate speech, misleading content, etc.)
- [ ] clear publisher identity (not anonymous/opaque)

**Original content advantage:**

Google News favours original reporting. As a verkkolehti producing original AI-written journalism from multiple sources, uutistenlukija is well-positioned for inclusion.

**Strategic implication:**

- If uutistenlukija includes category pages, topic summaries, or any editorial context, those strengthen the Google News case
- The explainer/context pieces discussed in the content strategy are directly relevant here
- Our original journalism model is a strong fit for Google News inclusion

**Action items:**

- [ ] create Google Publisher Center account post-launch
- [ ] generate and submit Google News sitemap
- [ ] ensure article pages have structured article metadata
- [ ] verify Search Console inclusion and monitor Google News performance report
- [ ] do NOT expect instant Top stories eligibility — build content quality over weeks
- [ ] Google News Showcase: evaluate post-traction if Google approaches or if the program is accessible for Finnish publishers

## 3) RSS / SYNDICATION PARTNERSHIPS WITH FINNISH PUBLISHERS

### Two distinct models

**Model A — Passive RSS ingest (current approach)**
Uutistenlukija uses publicly available RSS feeds as research sources for writing original journalism. No formal agreement needed since we produce original articles — RSS is a research input, not republished content.

**Model B — Active RSS partnership / feed agreement**
Publisher explicitly acknowledges or agrees to being cited as a source, possibly in exchange for:

- referral traffic guarantees
- attribution standards
- mutual promotion
- formal licensing (unlikely early-stage)

### Current RSS landscape status (from prior research)

- **Tier 1 (working feeds, currently active):** Yle, IS, IL, Turun Sanomat, Kauppalehti, Tekniikka & Talous (re-verify), Ars Technica, ScienceNews
- **Tier 2 (to test):** Muropaketti, Duodecim, Terveyskirjasto, New Scientist, The Verge, Variety
- **Tier 3 (avoid or needs resolution):** tiede.fi, tivi.fi, Alma Media domains with anti-AI notice

### RSS partnership opportunity matrix

| Publisher | Feed Status | Partnership Opportunity | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yle | Working concept feeds | Low — public broadcaster; implicit open use | Low |
| IS | Working feeds | Attribution partnership possible; commercial restriction | Medium |
| IL | Working feeds | High-risk for formal agreement (Alma Media) | High |
| Turun Sanomat | Working | Smaller regional; most open to informal relationship | Low-Medium |
| Kauppalehti | Working | Alma Media group risk; anti-AI notice | High |
| Tekniikka & Talous | Needs re-verify | Re-verify; worth formal contact if working | Medium |
| Pelaaja.fi | Tier 2 | Gaming niche; likely RSS-friendly | Low |
| Muropaketti | Tier 2 | Community/tech; open to partnerships | Low |
| Duodecim | Tier 2 | Medical/health; formal, conservative | Medium |

### Recommended RSS partnership approach

**Short-term (launch phase)**

- operate on passive RSS ingest from Tier 1 sources
- original journalism with proper source attribution
- log all sources clearly in internal allowlist

**Medium-term (3-6 months post-launch)**

After establishing traffic and referral data:

- reach out to 2-3 small/mid publishers with referral numbers
- propose informal reciprocal acknowledgment: "we feature your content prominently, here's the traffic we sent you"
- gauge interest in a formal content agreement
- best candidates: Turun Sanomat, Pelaaja.fi, Muropaketti (most open to partnerships)

**Longer-term**

- formal feed agreements or explicit permission letters from publishers
- publisher whitelisting with expanded snippet rights in exchange for attribution standards
- possibly co-branded category (e.g. "Teknologia — mukana Muropaketti")

## 4) TECH PARTNERSHIPS

### A. CDN — Cloudflare

**Why Cloudflare:**

- Free tier covers most launch needs (DDoS, caching, basic analytics)
- Polish + WebP image optimization already in the technical architecture plan
- Rocket Loader for JS performance
- Cloudflare Workers for edge logic if needed later
- Finnish/Nordic PoPs available for latency
- Free tier adequate until meaningful scale

**Partnership path:**

Cloudflare's Partner Network is primarily for agencies and resellers. For uutistenlukija, the relevant path is self-serve free/pro/business plan — not a formal partnership. Upgrade to Pro (€20/mo) or Business (€200/mo) when traffic justifies.

**Action items:**

- [ ] confirm Cloudflare is active and correctly configured (per technical architecture plan)
- [ ] enable Polish, WebP, Rocket Loader
- [ ] configure caching rules (HTML 15min TTL, static 30 days)
- [ ] monitor via Cloudflare Analytics (free, no consent required for basic stats)

---

### B. Analytics — GA4 + Microsoft Clarity

**GA4:**

- already confirmed connected
- implementation: fire after consent, custom events for signups/clicks

**Microsoft Clarity:**

- free session recording + heatmaps
- no data sampling
- privacy-compliant mode available
- fire after consent

**Potential future: Plausible Analytics**

**Plausible** is a privacy-first analytics alternative:

- no cookies by default
- GDPR-compliant by design — no consent banner needed for Plausible-only analytics
- from €9/mo; open source self-hostable
- worth evaluating as a simpler, consent-free analytics layer alongside GA4

**Action items:**

- [ ] GA4 production events verified (per launch checklist)
- [ ] Clarity installed post-consent
- [ ] evaluate Plausible as complementary privacy-first layer

---

### C. Ad Networks — for Finnish news inventory

**Priority 1: Google AdSense / Google Ad Manager**

**AdSense:**

- easiest entry point for display ad monetization
- low CPMs at small scale (€1-3 open auction typical)
- good for very early stage / before direct sales relationships

**Google Ad Manager (GAM):**

- more powerful ad server
- enables direct-sold ads alongside programmatic
- free up to 90 million monthly impressions
- supports header bidding

**Assessment:** Start with AdSense, migrate to GAM when direct deals emerge.

**Priority 2: Adform**

**Adform** is a Nordic/European ad tech platform with strong Finnish market penetration. Adform FLOW covers:

- demand-side platform (DSP)
- data management platform (DMP)
- supply-side platform (SSP)
- identity solution (ID Fusion, built for a post-cookie world)

**Assessment:** Adform is the most relevant non-Google ad tech partner for Finnish digital media. Their identity solution is specifically relevant given GDPR and cookie deprecation challenges. **Priority:** medium-term — when uutistenlukija has enough inventory to make direct-sold or PMP deals meaningful.

**Priority 3: IAB Finland relevance**

IAB Finland (`iab.fi`) is the Finnish digital advertising industry association:

- sets standards and guidelines for Finnish digital advertising
- publishes quarterly Finnish digital ad market data (via Kantar)
- working groups cover programmatic, viewability, brand safety, sustainability

**Assessment:** Not a day-one priority but worth tracking. Start reading IAB Finland reports for market data even before joining.

**Priority 4: Affiliate networks**

**Tradedoubler, Adtraction, AWIN** are the main affiliate networks active in Nordic/Finnish markets. Relevant for:

- Terveys & Hyvinvointi category (health product affiliates)
- Talous category (financial product affiliates)
- Teknologia & Tiede category (electronics affiliates)

**Assessment:** Low-friction revenue layer for certain categories. Worth investigating after 3-6 months when category traffic is established.

## Final Assessment

### Most valuable early partnerships

1. **Cloudflare** — already in play, just needs verification
2. **Google Publisher Center registration** — sets up for Showcase eligibility and Top stories
3. **Ampparit submission** — direct path to Finnish news-reading audience
4. **AdSense** — fastest route to any display revenue

### Medium-term partnership that matters most

**Adform** is the strategic ad tech partner for Finnish digital media at scale. Building toward that relationship (even if AdSense is the interim) is the right direction.

### The partnership that unlocks the most value but requires patience

**Referral-data-backed outreach to regional publishers** — once uutistenlukija demonstrably drives traffic, informal permission and eventually formal feed agreements become realistic. Traffic is the negotiating asset; build it first.

### What to avoid early

- aggressive outreach to major publishers (HS, Alma Media group) before traffic data exists and legal posture is very clearly settled
- joining publisher networks or trade bodies before there's a genuine business reason
- over-investing in Apple News or Showcase before Finnish market fit is confirmed
