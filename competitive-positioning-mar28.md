# Competitive Positioning — uutistenlukija.fi (March 28, 2026)

**Author:** Monica (via Discord bridge)
**Date:** 2026-03-28

## Closest Finnish Competitors

### 1. Yle Uutiset app
- **Advantage:** Default trusted news app, push distribution, speed, breadth, product polish, habit
- **Our gap:** Faster plain-language utility explainers tied to search intent ("what this means for me")

### 2. Ampparit
- **Advantage:** Source breadth, familiarity, quick headline scanning across many outlets
- **Our gap:** Higher-quality AI-assisted synthesis — not just links, but useful summaries, ranked context, topic pages around live Finnish issues

### 3. Google News (Finland)
- **Advantage:** Personalization, scale, discovery infrastructure, Google ecosystem habit
- **Our gap:** Finnish-first focus, local context depth, direct relationship with readers

### 4. Ilta-Sanomat / Iltalehti
- **Advantage:** Breaking-news velocity, homepage energy, mass-market headline demand
- **Our gap:** Less noise, more signal — fast articles explaining what changed and why it matters, without tabloid clutter

### 5. Helsingin Sanomat
- **Advantage:** Prestige, reporting depth, perceived authority for serious readers
- **Our gap:** Accessible depth at speed — selkokielinen, practical explainers easier to read and faster to publish

## The Gap We Should Own
**"The fastest Finnish site for clear, search-first explainers of live topics."**

- Yle owns trust and default app behavior
- Ampparit owns raw aggregation
- IS/IL own urgency and attention
- HS owns prestige and depth
- **We can win the space between them:** fast, useful, plain-language explainers built around real search intent

## One Concrete Action (90-day play)
Create a **daily explainer lane**: publish 1 search-driven utility explainer every day on a live Finnish topic.

Fixed format: **What happened → who it affects → what changes now → what to do / watch next.**

Consistent execution for 90 days builds a recognizable product edge instead of just another news feed.

## Notes
- This directly validates the "Selkokielinen talous" series already in production
- The format should become the pipeline's default template for policy/economy articles
- Topic selection should be driven by search intent data (Search Console + Monica's daily trending scan)
