# Finnish Online News Monetization Research

**Author:** Monica | **Date:** 2026-03-22 (päivitetty 2026-03-23) | **Status:** Complete

## Executive Summary

Finnish online news media monetize through **display advertising (primary), native sponsorships, and B2B services**. Unlike subscription-heavy models, the dominant strategy is **high-traffic ad-based revenue** with private marketplace (PMP) deals. Direct licensing (Google News Showcase) is emerging but limited to major publishers. As a verkkolehti producing original content, uutistenlukija.fi has stronger monetization potential than pure aggregators.

---

## 1. Display Advertising — The Primary Channel

### CPM/RPM Rates (Finland-Specific)

**For news content specifically:**
- News & Politics CPM average: €2.50-€2.90 (Q4 2024/Q1 2025)
- Google Ads CPM range: €3-€10 (standard), up to €10-€30+ (premium placements)
- Q1 2025 average Google CPM: €17.60 across all inventory
- CPC for Finnish market: €0.85 (desktop), €0.65 (mobile)

**Realistic RPM for news sites:** €4-€8 net (after platform cut)

### How Ampparit & Finnish News Sites Monetize Display

**Ampparit.com** (founded 2004, 250+ news sources) generates revenue through:
1. **Direct display advertising** on the site ("Mainosta Amppareissa")
2. **Programmatic sales** via Alma Media's SSP network (Xandr, Magnite, Adform, Google Ad Manager)
3. **B2B business intelligence tool** (Witpik) — professional media monitoring service

### Programmatic Market Structure

**Major SSPs for Finnish news inventory:**
- **Xandr** (Microsoft) — primary SSP for Sanoma & Alma media
- **Magnite, Adform, Pubmatic, Google Ad Manager** — secondary networks
- **~70% direct deals** still dominate Finnish news advertising (vs. 30% programmatic auction)
- **PMP (Private Marketplace) deals** heavily favored for brand safety

**Key insight:** Direct relationships with major media groups (Sanoma, Alma) matter more than auction-based sales in Finland. News inventory is premium and controlled.

## 2. Native Advertising & Sponsored Content

### Revenue Potential

**Kärkimedia** (Sanoma's native advertising arm) offers country-wide branded content across Finnish news sites with significant publisher revenue.

**Model:**
- Native ad articles published directly on news sites
- Clearly marked as "advertising" or "commercial collaboration" (Finnish law requirement)
- CPM: €12-€30+ (higher than display due to branding value)

### Regulations (Important)

- Finnish Consumer Ombudsman requires **clear identification** of sponsored content
- Must state: "Advertisement" or "Commercial collaboration" + brand name
- Trust level is high in Finland → sponsored content performs better

## 3. Newsletter Monetization

### Sponsored Newsletter Placements

Finnish publishers are growing email subscriber bases with personalized, segmented newsletters. Revenue models:

1. **Sponsored newsletter sections** — brands pay for dedicated sections
2. **Native ads in emails** — integrated content recommendations
3. **Affiliate links** — recommend products/services for commission
4. **Lead generation** — capture interested subscribers for B2B clients

**Pricing:** Not publicly listed, but typically €3,000-€15,000 per sponsored edition depending on subscriber count and engagement.

**Examples:** Keskisuomalainen increased subscriber retention 2-5% by implementing AI-driven personalized newsletters.

## 4. Direct Licensing (Google News Showcase)

### Google News Showcase in Finland

**Launched:** February 2024

**How it works:**
- Google pays publishers for readers' access to a limited amount of paywalled content
- Creates a "News Showcase panel" — curated content experience
- Revenue: Direct payments from Google (rates not public)

**Participating Finnish publishers:**
- Sanoma Media Finland (Helsingin Sanomat, Ilta-Sanomat)
- Keskisuomalainen
- Others

**Reality:** Limited to major publishers with premium content. As a verkkolehti producing original journalism, uutistenlukija.fi may qualify as it grows.

## 7. Realistic Revenue Mix for Online News Media

### Three-Tier Model (Most Common)

**Tier 1: Display + Programmatic (70% of revenue)**
- 500k monthly uniques @ 3 pages/session = 1.5M impressions
- €5 average RPM = **€7,500/month**
- Annual: **€90,000**

**Tier 2: Native Sponsorships + Newsletter (20%)**
- 2-3 sponsor placements/month @ €5,000 each = **€10,000-€15,000/month**
- Annual: **€120,000-€180,000**

**Tier 3: Affiliate + B2B Services (10%)**
- Finance/health category affiliate commissions = **€2,000-€5,000/month**
- Annual: **€24,000-€60,000**

**Total Annual Revenue (Conservative): €234,000-€330,000**
- *Requires: 500k-1M uniques/month, strong sponsorship relationships, affiliate partnerships*

## Critical Success Factors

- **Quality traffic** — News sites need clean, non-bot traffic. CPM varies wildly.
- **Direct relationships** — Sanoma/Alma direct deals beat open-market auctions
- **Newsletter list** — Email subscribers = predictable sponsorship revenue
- **Content credibility** — Native sponsorships + affiliate links only work if editorial trust is high
- **Regulatory compliance** — Clear sponsorship labeling per Finnish law = user trust
