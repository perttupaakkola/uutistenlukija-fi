## Part 1: Editorial Calendar Template

### Publishing Rhythm (Recommended for MVP Launch)

**Daily publishing targets:**

| Day | Article Count | Focus | Notes |
|-----|--------------|-------|-------|
| Mon | 4-5 | Strong start to week | Kotimaa, Politiikka, Talous focus |
| Tue | 4-5 | Sustained | Deep-dives, investigations, analysis |
| Wed | 4-5 | Mid-week momentum | Trending topics, viral angles |
| Thu | 5-6 | Peak day | Highest traffic day in Nordic markets |
| Fri | 4-5 | Weekend read prep | Long-form content, weekend guides |
| Sat | 2-3 | Lighter load | Lifestyle, culture, trending stories |
| Sun | 2-3 | Low volume | Recap/summary content, features |

**Why this pattern:**
- **Thursday peak:** Finns have more time to browse (end-of-week wind-down + evening commute)
- **Tuesday/Wednesday:** Post-weekend news accumulation, work distractions lower
- **Weekends:** Lower engagement (outdoor activity culture), but retain audience with quality long-form
- **Monday ramp:** Catch weekend news + work-week news (higher intent)

**Total weekly:** ~28-32 articles (4/day average) — manageable with curated aggregation

### Category Rotation (Weekly Theme)

Give each category prominence on a rotating basis to train readers on what to expect.

**Sample 4-week rotation:**

| Week | Monday Focus | Tuesday Focus | Wednesday Focus | Thursday Peak | Friday Focus |
|------|-------------|--------------|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| 1 | Kotimaa | Talous | Politiikka | Breaking | Tech Deep-dive |
| 2 | Talous | Teknologia | Kotimaa | Breaking | Viihde Feature |
| 3 | Politiikka | Urheilu | Terveys | Breaking | Kotimaa Analysis |
| 4 | Teknologia | Kotimaa | Talous | Breaking | Business Profile |

**Why rotating focus works:**
- Readers check specific days for specific categories
- Builds habit ("I always read Tech deep-dives on Friday")
- Helps with content planning
- Prevents category fatigue

## Part 2: Social Media Content Plan (By Platform)

### Platform 1: X (Twitter) — The Journalist's Channel

**Audience:** Journalists, politicians, business leaders, opinion makers — HIGH QUALITY but small (500k users)
**Traffic potential:** 3-8% of daily visitors (high intent)
**Content style:** Breaking news, hot takes, threaded analysis, real-time commentary

**Best-performing formats on X:**

1. **Breaking News Tweet + Link**
   - Format: Emoji + headline + 3 bullet points + link
   - Timing: Within 5 minutes of story breaking
   - Length: Keep body under 200 characters

2. **Thread (Investigation/Analysis)**
   - Format: Numbered thread, 5-8 tweets
   - Best posting time: Tuesday-Thursday, 8-9 AM or 5-7 PM
   - Drive engagement: Each tweet is quotable/RT-able

3. **Quote Tweet + Commentary**
   - Capitalize on trending conversations
   - Add original insight (don't just RT)
   - Timing: Within 1 hour of trend peak

4. **Data Visualization**
   - Visual + 1-2 sentence context + link
   - Best for: Economics, politics, demographic stories

5. **Question to Audience**
   - Drive engagement, gather story ideas
   - Timing: Off-peak (avoid competing for attention)

**X Posting Schedule:**

| Frequency | Timing | Content Type |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Morning (7-9 AM) | 1 tweet | Breaking news / trending reaction |
| Midday (12-1 PM) | 0-1 tweets | Data viz OR question to audience |
| Evening (5-7 PM) | 1-2 tweets | Main story of day + follow-up |
| Late evening (9 PM) | 0-1 tweets | Thread or explainer (weekend option) |

**Weekly total:** 5-7 tweets/week (quality > quantity)

### Platform 2: Facebook — The Broad Reach

**Audience:** Older demographics (35+), families, general population — WIDE but declining engagement
**Traffic potential:** 10-15% of daily visitors (but lower quality, lower intent)
**Content style:** Stories, images, community questions, lighter engagement

**Best-performing formats on Facebook:**

1. **Image Post with Headline + CTA**
   - Use article hero image or custom graphic
   - Headline < 8 words
   - CTA: "Read more", "Learn details"

2. **Personal/Human Angle Story**
   - Favor emotional/human interest angles
   - Include quote in post if possible

3. **Poll/Question**
   - Polls increase engagement significantly
   - Keep to 4 options max
   - Link to full article in comment

4. **Video Snippet**
   - 30-60 second video, subtitled
   - Timing: Afternoons (1-3 PM) or evening (5-7 PM)

5. **Carousel (Multiple Images)**
   - Swipeable gallery: "5 things you should know about [topic]"
   - Best for listicles, how-tos, galleries

**Facebook Posting Schedule:**

| Day | Frequency | Time | Content |
|-----|-----------|------|---------|
| Mon-Fri | 1 post/day | 7-8 AM or 5-6 PM | News + image |
| Sat-Sun | 0-1 posts | 10-11 AM | Lighter/lifestyle story |
| Any day | 2-3/week | 1-3 PM | Poll or engagement post |

**Weekly total:** 5-7 posts/week

### Platform 3: Reddit/r/suomi — The Community Nerds

**Audience:** Tech-savvy, educated, skeptical, discussion-oriented — SMALL but extremely engaged
**Traffic potential:** 1-3% of daily visitors (but highest-quality, most loyal readers)
**Content style:** Thoughtful, data-backed, discussion-friendly, not clickbait

**Best-performing formats on Reddit:**

1. **Straightforward Link + Context Comment**
   - Never just spam link (gets downvoted)
   - Always comment with context/question
   - Engage authentically in discussion

2. **"Thought this was interesting..." with Discussion Hook**
   - Frame as genuine discovery, not promotion
   - Ask open question to drive discussion

3. **Crosspost to Relevant Subreddit**
   - Post to r/suomi AND r/nordics (if relevant)
   - Don't crosspost everything (too spammy)
   - Only 1-2 stories/week

4. **Data Visualization Post**
   - Reddit loves OC charts
   - Always credit sources

5. **AMA-Style (Ask Me Anything)**
   - Post quarterly or bi-monthly
   - Builds community trust

**Reddit Posting Strategy:**

| Frequency | Day | Time | Content |
|-----------|-----|------|---------|
| 1-2 posts/week | Tue-Thu | 8-9 AM or 5-7 PM | Link + honest context |
| 0-1 posts/week | Any | Best-performing time | Data viz or discussion hook |
| 0-1 posts/quarter | Any | Any | AMA or community feedback |

**Critical Reddit rules:**
- ❌ NEVER use Reddit as a broadcast channel
- ❌ Don't post multiple stories in one day
- ❌ Don't delete comments that criticize you
- ✅ Engage authentically in discussions
- ✅ Acknowledge when you don't know something
- ✅ Give genuine credit to other sources

**Weekly total:** 1-2 posts/week max

## Part 3: Finnish Newsletter Content Format

### What Top Finnish Newsletters Look Like

Based on best practices from Finnish media (Aamulehti, Kaleva, regional outlets) and European news organizations:

**Key characteristics:**
- **Length:** 600-900 words (longer than English newsletters)
- **Tone:** Personal but professional (warm, not stiff)
- **Structure:** Story-first (not a listicle), with context sections
- **Frequency:** Daily (5-6 days/week for news)
- **Language:** Standard Finnish, clear and direct
- **Visual:** Minimal (one hero image or none)

### Newsletter Structure Template (For Daily Digest)

**SUBJECT LINE (critical — tested on Nordic markets)**

Rule: `[Number/Question/Personal pronoun] + [Value] + [Emoji]`

Examples:
- ✅ "Mitä sinun pitää tietää tänään" (5 things you need to know)
- ✅ "3 asiaa jotka muuttuvat ensi viikolla" (3 things changing next week)
- ✅ "Tämän viikon trendit — analyysi" (This week's trends — analysis)
- ✅ "Miksi suomalaiset ovat huolissaan juuri nyt?" (Why Finns are worried right now)
- ❌ Avoid: ALL CAPS, emoji spam (limit to 1), clickbait ("SHOCKING")

**EMAIL HEADER (First 200 characters visible in preview)**

```
Hei [First Name]! 👋

Tänään päivitämme kolme suurinta uutista
sekä analyysin siitä, mikä muuttuu ensi viikolla.
```

**SECTION 1: LEAD STORY (The Big News)**

```
# PÄÄUUTINEN: [Headline]

[Hero image or pull quote]

**Mikä tapahtui?**
2-3 sentence summary

**Miksi se on tärkeää?**
2-3 sentences on impact/context

**Mitä seuraavaksi?**
What to expect next

[READ FULL STORY →]
```
Length: 150-200 words. Tone: Straight news, no opinion.

**SECTION 2: SECONDARY STORY**

```
# TOISEKSI SUURIN: [Headline]

[One paragraph of context]

[READ MORE →]
```
Length: 50-100 words. Different category from lead.

**SECTION 3: TRENDING / ANALYSIS**

```
# TÄNÄÄN TRENDI: [Topic]

"Why Finns care about this right now"

[2-3 paragraphs of analysis or explanation]

[DEEP DIVE: Full article →]
```
Length: 150-250 words. Tone: Explanatory.

**SECTION 4: OPTIONAL — WEEKEND PICK (Friday only)**

```
## VIIKONLOPUN LUKU 📖

"A long-form story worth your time"

[1-2 sentence teaser]

Long-form stories Finns love:
- Profiles of interesting people
- Explanations of complex topics
- "Why does Finland do X?" pieces
- Historical context pieces

[READ WEEKEND FEATURE →]
```

**SECTION 5: FOOTER**

```
Mitä haluat lukea ensi viikolla?

Reply to this email — kertoa meille aiheista
jotka kiinnostavat sinua. Kuuntelemme!

[MANAGE PREFERENCES] [UNSUBSCRIBE]
```

### Newsletter Tone Guide (Finnish Context)

**✅ DO:**
- Write like you're explaining to a smart friend
- Use "sinä" (you) — personal
- "Tänään" (today), "juuri nyt" (right now) — sense of urgency
- Questions in headlines ("Mitä tapahtui?")
- Admit uncertainty ("We don't know yet, but...")

**❌ DON'T:**
- Use formal "te" (unless premium business segment)
- Marketing speak ("revolutionary", "exclusive")
- Emoji spam (max 1-2 per email)
- Jargon without explanation
- Fake excitement ("OMG", "shocking")

### Newsletter Sending Schedule

**Segment 1: Daily Digest (Free)**
- Sent: Every weekday 7:00 AM Helsinki time
- Length: 600-800 words
- Format: 3-4 stories + trending topic

**Segment 2: Trending Now (Free, Young Users)**
- Sent: Mon, Wed, Fri at 5:00 PM
- Length: 300-400 words
- Format: 2-3 trending stories + casual tone

**Segment 3: Business & Tech (Premium)**
- Sent: Weekday 8:00 AM
- Length: 500-700 words
- Format: Deep-dive Talous/Tech story + market data

**Segment 4: Weekend Read (Free)**
- Sent: Friday 6:00 PM
- Length: 1-2 long-form features
- Format: One feature + one profile
