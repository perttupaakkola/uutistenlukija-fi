# User Personas — Uutistenlukija.fi

**Author:** Monica | **Date:** 2026-03-22 | **Status:** Complete
**Sources:** Reuters Institute 2024-2025, Sanoma/Alma media insights

---

## Persona 1: The Direct Accessor

**Age:** 35-55 | **Digital skill:** High | **Income:** €50k-€80k+
**Device:** Desktop/Mobile (50/50) | **Trust level:** Very High

### Behavior
- Reads online news **daily**, multiple times (average 3+ sessions)
- **Avoids social media** for news — prefers direct access to publisher websites/apps
- Willing to **pay for quality** — 20% of Finns pay for digital news, and this segment is 40% of that group
- Values **credibility & depth** — reads full articles, not headlines
- Strong interest in: **Kotimaa, Politiikka, Talous, Urheilu**

### Pain Points
- **Paywall fragmentation:** Has to pay separate subscriptions to Helsingin Sanomat, Kauppalehti, regional outlets
- **No unified access:** Can't bundle multiple premium outlets affordably
- **27% of unpaywalled readers** would subscribe if bundled options existed at reasonable price
- Frustrated by having to jump between 3-5 different sites to stay informed

### What Would Convert
- Single news destination with original articles covering all major Finnish news topics (no paywalls to clear)
- Email newsletter with curated daily digest (saves 30 min/day of browsing)
- Category filtering — jump straight to Talous or Politiikka without clutter
- Premium tier option later (e.g., deep analysis pieces, ad-free)

## Persona 2: The Young Social Native

**Age:** 18-24 | **Digital skill:** Expert | **Income:** €20k-€35k (students/early career)
**Device:** Smartphone 90% | **Trust level:** Moderate (skeptical of AI-generated news)

### Behavior
- **44% use social media as primary news source** (TikTok, Instagram, Reddit)
- Only **79% read online news weekly** (vs 90%+ older groups)
- **Avoids news sometimes** (39% of this age group) due to negative mood impact
- Watches news videos heavily (75% consume video news)
- Interest: **Teknologia, Viihde, Urheilu, trending topics**
- Uses AI chatbots & user comments for fact-checking

### Pain Points
- **Paywalls turn them away** — 18-25 year-olds bypass paywalls via social media
- **Information overload** — too much noise, prefer curated summaries over firehose
- **Misinformation risk** — 66% TikTok, 55% X seen as sources of false info
- **News avoidance** — 39% occasionally avoid news due to overwhelm or negative impact
- Prefers short-form content but wants **depth when interested**

### What Would Convert
- Mobile-first design with clean interface (no overwhelming banner ads)
- TL;DR summaries for quick-read articles (hook to full story)
- Trending/viral section — what's breaking in Tech, Entertainment, Sports
- Social sharing buttons prominent (easy to share on TikTok, Instagram)
- No paywall (will bounce if hit wall immediately)
- Fact-check labels on stories (builds trust with skeptical youth)

## Persona 3: The Finnish Professional/Entrepreneur

**Age:** 25-40 | **Digital skill:** High | **Income:** €60k-€120k+
**Device:** Mobile primary, desktop secondary | **Trust level:** High

### Behavior
- Reads **business/tech news daily** — invested in Finland's startup ecosystem
- Interests: **Talous, Teknologia, some Politiikka**
- **25% of this age group** willing to pay for bundled news if affordable
- Likely reads: Kauppalehti, Talouselämä, Tekniikka&Talous + international sources
- Uses news for **business intelligence**, decision-making
- Mobile commute = news while traveling

### Pain Points
- **Scattered sources:** Tech news on Medium, business on Kauppalehti, politics on HS, international on LinkedIn
- **International gap:** Finnish news media miss Silicon Valley/EU tech trends
- **Paywall costs** add up fast when subscribing to multiple verticals
- **Lack of curated finance/startup content** — wants analysis, not just headlines
- **Time-poor** — needs digests, not raw firehose

### What Would Convert
- Business/Tech category curated with best sources (domestic + international)
- "Startup Digest" weekly or daily email (Finnish startup scene + funding news)
- Affiliate opportunities (investment platforms, business tools) embedded naturally
- Price point €2-€5/month would be attractive for bundled access
- Newsletter segmentation: One for Tech, one for Finance/Markets
- Trending topics in business (IPOs, M&A, AI applications)

## Persona 4: The Nordic Retiree

**Age:** 60-75 | **Digital skill:** Moderate | **Income:** Pension + savings
**Device:** Desktop primary, tablet secondary | **Trust level:** Highest

### Behavior
- **66% read print news weekly** (still buys physical newspaper)
- **Also reads online news** (81% reach weekly digital in 2025)
- Heavy interest in: **Politiikka, Talous, Kotimaa, Urheilu, Terveys**
- **Very high trust in news** — prefers major outlets like HS, Aamulehti
- Reads for **depth & context**, not speed
- Strong local news interest (unlike younger groups)

### Pain Points
- **Print declining but still paying** — shifting to digital is frustrating
- **UI complexity** — large text, simple navigation needed
- **Paywalls are confusing** — doesn't understand why some articles free, others not
- **Overwhelming ads on news sites** — adds to visual clutter
- **Missing local/regional coverage** — wants hometown news covered alongside national
- **Tech trust gap** — skeptical of AI-written news

### What Would Convert
- Large, readable text options (accessibility matters)
- Simple, clean design (no infinite scroll, clear structure)
- Regional news hub — hometown + national bundled
- Local news section prominent (unlike younger users, local content is key)
- Health/wellness category curated (strong interest in Terveys)
- "No paywall" philosophy — all content free, ad-supported
- Print-like reading experience — article-focused, not feed-based

## Persona 5: The Bundled Subscriber

**Age:** 30-50 | **Digital skill:** High | **Income:** €45k-€90k
**Device:** Mobile primary | **Trust level:** High

### Behavior
- **Willing to subscribe if price is right** — €10-€15/month for bundled access
- Currently pays for **Helsingin Sanomat OR regional paper**, not multiple
- Would trade paywall hassle for **single subscription across multiple outlets**
- Reads: **Kotimaa, Talous, some Urheilu + niche interest**
- Wants **one login, multiple sources**, similar to how Spotify/Netflix work

### Pain Points
- **Subscribe to HS? Then miss Alma Media's outlets (Iltalehti, Kauppalehti)**
- **No combined premium tier** — services like Kaikki+ exist but incomplete
- **Overlap frustration:** Same stories on multiple outlets = paying for duplicates
- **Admin burden:** Managing multiple subscriptions, logins, payment methods
- **Unclear value proposition:** What's unique about each outlet?

### What Would Convert
- "Uutistenlukija Premium" tier at €4.99-€7.99/month
  - Headline access to all major Finnish outlets (via licensing deals with Sanoma/Alma)
  - Ad-free reading (or low-ad)
  - Email newsletter daily digest
- Single sign-on (one login for all content)
- Personalized feed based on interests (auto-suggest Talous if you read business)
- Save articles for later, reading time tracking
- Sharing 1-2 articles/month with non-subscribers (viral loop)

## Summary: Target Priority

### MVP Launch Priority (Free)
1. **Direct Accessor** — Already pays for news, appreciates quality original coverage
2. **Young Social Native** — Needs phone-first, shareable, no paywall
3. **Finnish Professional** — High intent, valuable for sponsors/affiliates

### Post-MVP (Monetization)
4. **Nordic Retiree** — Ad-supported, high engagement, local news focus
5. **Bundled Subscriber** — Premium tier @ €5-€8/mo with ecosystem partnerships

## Design Implications (For Sara)

**Homepage layout should work for all:**
- **Direct Accessor:** Top stories + category quick links (quick scanning)
- **Young Social Native:** Hero image + trending section + share buttons prominent
- **Professional:** Category tabs (Talous/Tech) immediately visible
- **Retiree:** Large text option, local news block
- **Bundled Subscriber:** "Premium available" CTA, subscription perks highlighted

**Newsletter strategy:**
- Segment: Business digest (Professionals), Culture+Entertainment (Youth), Regional (Retirees)
- Each persona gets different subject lines, content depth, CTA

**Monetization alignment:**
- Direct Accessors & Bundled Subscribers → Premium subscription tier
- Young Social Natives → Low-friction display ads + affiliate links
- Professionals → Finance affiliate programs (Nordnet, OKX links)
- Retirees → Straightforward display ads (trust-heavy brands)
